🎟️ The HTOTP Analogy (5th Grade Level)

The Magical Lockbox

The Nonce (The Shiny Sticker): When you ask for an OTP, we print a shiny sticker that can only be used once. If you hand it to the bouncer and successfully get in, they rip it up.

HKDF Key Derivation (Play-Doh Mixing): The server has a huge lump of blue Play-Doh (Master Key). For every request, it mixes a tiny piece of it with unique glitter (your phone number + sticker number) to make a brand new, one-time color just for you.

Stateless Attempt Counter (3 Lives): You receive a Magic Sealed Envelope (requestToken) with 3 lives drawn on the outside. Every time you guess the OTP wrong, the bouncer crosses out one life with a sharpie and seals it again. At 0, the game is over.
